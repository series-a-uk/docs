## Duplicates on the calendar

Duplicate posts appear when two content strategies write into one calendar (e.g. a strategy created twice, one minute apart). Dedupe by title, keep the oldest copy, verify with a re-list.

## Taplio resurrection

Taplio re-sync (every 15 minutes) can re-import deleted posts minutes later with new ids. After any delete: re-list and verify by body-text match. Delete twice if needed. If a post keeps resurrecting, the fix belongs at the Taplio source (app.taplio.com), not the calendar layer.

## Lead filtering lies

`GET /campaigns?lead_email=` works; the UI list filter does not. Always verify leads through the API.

## Cross-workspace writes

A workspace key can sometimes READ another workspace but rejects WRITES with "This workspace key is not authorized for the requested workspace_id". Use the admin key for multi-workspace work, with explicit `--workspace`.

## Timezones

Calendar times are UTC; LinkedIn and Taplio display them in local time (+1/+2). The same post can look like a different day in different views. Dedupe by body text, not by date alone.
