House rules for everything Series-X produces: client copy, LinkedIn posts, emails, videos, docs.

## Non-negotiables

- **British English** everywhere (except client-specific overrides, e.g. Chris Thomas = US spelling).
- **No em dashes.** No double dashes either. Use a comma, colon or full stop.
- **No exclamation marks** in professional copy.
- **Banned marketing words:** unlock, empower, transformative, journey, seamless, revolutionary, cutting-edge, leverage (as a verb), intelligent (as marketing), 10x, game-changer, passive income, while you sleep.
- **No "honest read" / "honestly" / "to be straight" framing.** State the point directly.
- Address people by first name. One decisive recommendation, not option menus.

## Tone of voice

Confession-before-credential. Concrete scenes and numbers. Short sentences with hard stops. Anti-sell by default. The goal of outreach is the meeting, not the sale.

## Brand assets

**Never regenerate, redraw or approximate a client logo.** If the asset is missing, ask for it. Client-supplied artwork is saved pristine under the project `brand/` directory; transparency is derived (white-to-alpha extraction), never redrawn.
